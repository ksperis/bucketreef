# Your Portal Profile

Open **Profile** from Portal to change your personal appearance, language,
notifications, and sign-in security while keeping the current Portal project
available in the navigation.

## Personal preferences

You can change your avatar, language, theme, and default workspace. If quota
email notifications are available for your account, you can also choose whether
to receive them.

Profile images can come from your uploaded image, your linked identity, or
Gravatar depending on your preference. Choose initials if you do not want an
external profile image to be loaded.

## Sign-in security

Use **Security** to manage the sign-in methods available to your own account,
including password when applicable, passkeys, recovery codes, open sessions,
and linked external identities.

If you renew recovery codes, previous codes stop working and existing sessions
are signed out. Save the new codes before leaving the delivery screen.

## What the profile does not change

Your profile does not change your Portal project role or your permissions on a
Storage Space. If a project or space is missing, use [Troubleshooting](help/troubleshooting.md)
or [Help requests](help-requests.md).

A Portal Manager role remains a Portal responsibility and does not grant access
to the Manager workspace.
