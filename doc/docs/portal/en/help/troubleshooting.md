# Portal Troubleshooting

Use this page when you cannot find a project or Storage Space, open files, or
complete a Portal action.

## Try these checks first

1. Confirm the project selected in Portal.
2. Return to **Spaces** and open the Storage Space again.
3. Refresh the page if someone has just changed your access.
4. Read the message beside a disabled action or the error shown after a failed
   action.
5. If the problem continues, open [Help requests](../help-requests.md) when that
   workflow is available, or contact your administrator.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to check |
|---|---|
| Portal or a project is missing | You must be a member of that Portal project. |
| A Storage Space is missing | Make sure the correct project is selected and ask the owner or Portal Manager whether you should have access. |
| Upload or delete is unavailable | Your role on the space may be read-only, the space may be archived, or the action may be unavailable for the project. |
| Previous versions are missing | The space may not keep versions, or your access may not allow version history. |
| Cannot invite someone | Your Portal role or project delegation may require a Help request instead of a direct change. |
| External link or external-tool access is unavailable | The project administrator may have disabled that function. |

When reporting a problem, include the project name, Storage Space, file or
action, time, and exact error text. Never send secret keys or passwords.
