# Start Here

Portal is the workspace for working with the storage projects and **Storage
Spaces** assigned to you.

## Your first visit

1. Open `/portal` and choose the project you were given access to.
2. Open **Spaces**.
3. Choose a Storage Space and open **Files**.
4. Upload a small test file if your role allows it, then download it again.
5. Use **Collaborators** when you need to understand who can access the space.
6. Check **Storage health** when you need to understand capacity or alerts.

You do not need to choose S3 accounts, endpoints, or execution identities for
normal Portal work.

## Portal roles

- **Portal User** works with the spaces and actions granted to them.
- **Portal Manager** has additional project responsibilities inside Portal.

Portal Manager does not mean access to the separate Manager workspace.
